FAQ

Questions businesses ask

What's the difference between Standard and Extended?

Standard covers digital use — websites, social media, email and blogs — up to 500,000 impressions. Extended adds print (brochures, posters, packaging, signage) and paid advertising with unlimited impressions.

What does an Exclusive buyout mean?

You get Extended rights and we remove the image from sale. Previous non-exclusive licenses sold before your buyout remain valid; we disclose how many existed before you buy.

Are people in the photos model-released?

Each image shows its release status. Only released images are cleared for advertising use. (In this prototype, release info is a placeholder.)

Can my agency license on behalf of a client?

Yes. Enter your client's business name at checkout and the license certificate is issued to them. Summit Team includes 5 seats for agency staff.

Do subscription downloads expire?

Licenses you've downloaded are perpetual even if you cancel. Unused monthly downloads roll over for one month.

Can I cancel or change plans?

Yes, anytime from your account. Annual plans are billed upfront and can be cancelled at renewal.

Do you offer refunds?

Unused single-image purchases can be refunded within 14 days if the file hasn't been downloaded.

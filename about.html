About us

Shot outside. Licensed for business.

Fernline Media is a curated library of outdoor adventure photography made for the businesses that run, sell and promote the outdoors.

Big stock sites are built for everyone, which means outdoor imagery gets buried under staged studio shots. We focus on one thing: authentic hiking, paddling, climbing, biking, snow and fishing photography — with licenses small businesses can understand without a lawyer.
